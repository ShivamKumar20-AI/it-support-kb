# Monitor shows no signal, or a USB device is not detected

**Category:** Peripherals | **Audience:** First-line support

## Symptom
A monitor shows "No signal" or is blank, or a keyboard, mouse or USB device does nothing when plugged in.

## Likely causes
- Loose or faulty cable, or wrong input selected on the monitor
- Faulty port, dock or adapter
- Power saving or USB selective suspend
- Driver issue

## Steps — monitor
1. Check the monitor has power and the correct **input source** (HDMI, DisplayPort) selected.
2. Reseat the video cable at both ends. Try a different cable and a different port.
3. Swap in a known working monitor to see whether the screen or the PC is at fault.
4. Press **Windows key + P** and choose Extend or Duplicate. Check Settings > System > Display > Detect.
5. If the PC uses a dock, disconnect and reconnect the dock and check its power supply.

## Steps — USB device
1. Plug the device into a different port, ideally directly on the PC rather than a hub.
2. Try the device on another PC. If it also fails, replace the device.
3. Check **Device Manager** for unknown devices or warnings. Reinstall the driver (article 06).
4. Restart the PC and retest.

## Escalate when
- The fault follows the cable, dock or device: raise a replacement
- Port damage is visible on the PC
- Several ports have failed (possible motherboard issue)

## Notes
Change one thing at a time. Swapping the cable, the port and the monitor all at once tells you nothing about which was at fault.
